Good to know

Your questions, answered honestly.

What does it cost to offer services?+
Free during launch — the AED 30/month listing fee starts later, and we'll give you 30 days' notice before it does. The only other cost is a 10% platform fee on bookings the customer pays through Trove by card. Direct bookings — bank transfer or cash between you and the customer — carry no commission.
How do I get paid for bookings paid through Trove?+
By bank transfer from Serein Consultancy LLC, the company behind Trove,, so look for that name on your statement. Your fee — the price less the 10% platform fee — is provisional until the service date has passed and a 3-day window for any complaint or cancellation has closed; it is then paid on the next payout day, every other Tuesday, the same days Trove pays its makers. Add your bank details under Payouts in your dashboard; each fee there shows when it’s payable and, once sent, the date and the transfer reference.
Who is responsible for the service?+
The provider performs it, always: Trove doesn't perform or supervise the work. Who you contract with depends on how you pay. Pay through Trove by card and Trove is your contracting party for that booking and refunds you in full if the service isn't delivered. Settle directly and the arrangement, and any refund, is between you and the provider. The Provider Agreement and the Services Terms both say so plainly — read them before you apply or book.
Why can't I see the customer's number straight away?+
The same privacy stance as the rest of Trove: contact details change hands only when there's a confirmed arrangement to use them for. The mobile is released the moment you confirm; the email address stays with Trove.
What can I offer?+
Anything in the twelve categories above — creative work at people's homes, and brand, content or shop support for makers. Regulated trades are out of scope: no food or catering, no beauty or skin treatments, and advice-only where a profession is licensed.
Where does it work?+
Dubai and Abu Dhabi, like the rest of Trove. Each service says where it happens — at the customer's place, at your studio, or remote for work like design, copy and coaching.
I sell pieces on Trove — can I offer services too?+
Yes, and it's a natural pairing: one account can run a maker shop and a services practice side by side. A potter who sells bowls can host hand-building workshops; a photographer who shoots for makers can list family shoots at home.
Do customers need an account to book?+
No — anyone can send a request with just a name, email and UAE mobile. We email you a private link to follow the booking, pay for it if you chose card, and cancel it before the service day. Signed-in customers also see every request on this page under "Your bookings".
