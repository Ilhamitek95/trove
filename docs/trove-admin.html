Return requests

Buyers pick the items (and units) going back, a reason and photos, within 15 days of delivery. Approving books the courier collection; the refund goes back to their card once the courier has collected the item, and only then do the shops' credits reverse. The AED 30 collection fee applies only to 'changed my mind' on orders of AED 200 and below — tick or untick it to override. It is charged per request, so pieces sent back together pay it once. The original delivery fee is refunded only when the whole order comes back for a fault (the request that completes the order, with every returned unit a fault) — the second tick box overrides that either way. Declines go back to the buyer with your reason.
