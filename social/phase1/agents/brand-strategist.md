# Trove Phase 1 recruitment: campaign and language

Brand strategist, 19 Oct 2026. For the ten grid posts from 21 Oct to 17 Nov. It follows `social/BRIEF.md`. No line here is taken from the website.

## 1. Audience portraits

These are composite planning portraits, never to be shown as real people.

**The one everyone asks (has never sold anything).** She lives in Mirdif and stitches a name onto every new baby's blanket in her circle.
- *Believes:* "It's just something I do for people I love. Who would pay for it?"
- *Holds back:* It isn't good enough to charge for. Licences, couriers and pricing feel like someone else's world.
- *Would move her:* Recognition from outside her circle. Hearing that four pieces is a shop, the photos and the courier are taken care of, and she sets her own pace.

**The weekend restorer (provider, has never charged).** An engineer who mends neighbours' chairs in his building's storeroom.
- *Believes:* "I'm not a carpenter. I just like fixing things."
- *Holds back:* No company and no workshop. He would feel like an impostor naming a price.
- *Would move him:* Repair named as a craft, bookings arriving in one place, and an invitation to "the one who brings things back".

**The market-stall ceramicist (established).** Works from home in Khalifa City and sells at markets and through DMs.
- *Believes:* "I'm a real maker. I just can't run the business side as well."
- *Holds back:* Losing her brand, being tied to one place, yet another app.
- *Would move her:* Photography, courier and buyer care taken off her plate. Also that it isn't exclusive, that she'll be in good company, and the Founding maker title.

**The family calligrapher (Emirati, has never sold anything).** In Abu Dhabi. She letters every wedding card and Eid greeting in the family.
- *Believes:* "This is for family, not business."
- *Holds back:* Privacy (no face online) and modesty.
- *Would move her:* Being shown through her hands and work only, an Arabic invitation, and her craft treated as home and heritage rather than a "side hustle".

**The party stylist (provider, informal).** Styles every Diwali, birthday and Christmas in her building, and is paid in thank-yous.
- *Believes:* "Anyone could do this."
- *Holds back:* No portfolio, no idea how to price, and the fear of "selling to friends".
- *Would move her:* Styling named as a service, the season of celebrations arriving, and services giftable from December.

## 2. The insight

> The most gifted hands in the UAE don't think of themselves as makers; they think of themselves as the one everyone asks. Their work already lives in other people's homes as favours and gifts, and what holds them back is not talent but that nobody has told them it deserves a home of its own.

## 3. Campaign platform

| # | Line | Arabic (MSA). Native review needed | Why it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Made here. Made home.** | صُنِعَ هنا، فصارَ بيتاً. | Three meanings: made in the UAE, made at home, and how a place becomes home. |
| B | **Home is handmade.** | البيتُ تصنعُه الأيدي. | The plainest statement of the territory, but possibly a common phrase (VERIFY). |
| C | **You're the one everyone asks.** | الكلُّ يقصدُك أنت. | It names a moment hobbyists already live, and it turns into a nomination. |
| D | **For the hands that make a house a home.** | للأيدي التي تجعلُ البيتَ بيتاً. | It includes restorers and stylists, but the idiom is well worn. |
| E | **A home for what you make.** | بيتٌ لِما تصنعُه يداك. | Clear about Trove's role, but descriptive rather than rallying. |

**Recommendation: A, with C as the recruitment hook underneath it.**
- "Here" places it in Dubai and Abu Dhabi without flags or slogans.
- "Home" carries the owner's homely territory.
- It fits a bowl, a mended chair, an Eid table and a lettered card equally.
- It never mentions money or business, so people who have never sold anything aren't put off.
- In December it becomes the reason to buy.

C does the recruiting. "You're the one everyone asks" speaks to the maker; "Who's the one everyone asks?" is the nomination. The unvocalised Arabic suffix ك reads for both men and women, so no line has to pick a gender.

**The "UAE as home" territory.** Home here means two things at once:
- For Emirati families, crafts such as talli and sadu handed down for generations (VERIFY wording).
- For residents of a hundred-plus nationalities, a home they built, often carrying a stitch or skill from elsewhere into life here.

Its textures are specific: the first cool evenings when doors open and people host again; Diwali lamps and Christmas trees in one tower; the Eid table; the wedding card an aunt lettered. We stay with those objects and hands.

Four rules:
- No abstract "community" talk.
- Never "expats".
- Never suggest everyone arrived from elsewhere.
- "Support" means practical help (photographs, courier, a shop, a spotlight), never charity.

## 4. Language system

**What we call the people:**
- By their craft first: embroiderer, restorer, calligrapher, stylist.
- As a group: "makers" for pieces, and "the people who make, mend, style and teach" for everyone.
- The programme is "Founding maker".
- "Provider" stays in forms, never in headlines.

**What we call Trove's role:** "a home for your work". We talk about the platform, as the owner asked, through verbs rather than the word "platform": *we photograph, we give it a shop, we look after the buyer, a courier comes to your door.*

**We use:**
1. home
2. made here (always with Dubai and Abu Dhabi named)
3. hands
4. the one everyone asks
5. your pieces, your work
6. make, mend, style, teach
7. neighbour
8. four lovely pieces is a shop
9. at your own pace, and "There's no rush"
10. the people who make things here

**We never use:**
1. vendor, seller, merchant, supplier
2. side hustle, monetise, passive income
3. entrepreneur, boss, small business owner
4. "just a hobby" in our own voice (we only quote it back)
5. empower, unleash, ecosystem, journey, artisanal
6. hurry, limited, spots, last chance
7. first, only, leading, "the UAE's Etsy"
8. expats
9. "giving back", "support local" as charity talk
10. products, inventory, content, instant, mass-produced

**Before and after:**
- *Before:* "Sell on Trove and keep 60% of every sale!"
  *After:* "Your candles are already on every table. Let a few more homes have one." (The 60% goes into one caption.)
- *Before:* "Turn your passion into profit and grow your small business."
  *After:* "You call it a hobby. Your friends call you first."
- *Before:* "Service providers: list your services today and get more bookings!"
  *After:* "You're the one who mends the chair, letters the cards and sets the Eid table. There's a place on Trove for that too."

## 6. Recruitment mechanics beyond the grid

1. **"Who's the one everyone asks?"**
   - Post 5, plus a Story question sticker all month. People tag publicly or send a name by DM.
   - A real person writes one gentle note to each nominee and names the nominator only with permission.
   - Add "Did someone send you?" to /apply to track it (VERIFY).
2. **A Founding maker title worth showing.**
   - Badge and spotlight, plus a portrait session at the maker's own bench with the in-house photographer. The photos are theirs to use (VERIFY rights).
   - A printed card for the wall, and a collab post so their own circle sees it.
   - "The first 20 to 30" stays internal; never count places in public.
3. **Bring-one-thing afternoons.** One in Dubai and one in Abu Dhabi, in the week of 5 Nov. The photographer shoots a piece and the team helps with the ten-minute form. This takes away the fear of applying.
4. **Downtown Design postcards.** The team walks the fair (no stand assumed) handing out "I know someone who makes ____" cards with a QR code to /apply. Visitors become nominators. No fair logo, no implied partnership.
5. **"Hands, not faces."** Any maker can be shown through hands and work only. We say so in Arabic Stories and DMs. It opens the door for home-based makers who value their privacy.

## 8. VERIFY

1. **"No company needed to start"** (posts 2 and 10): needs a UAE business adviser's sign-off before posting.
2. **Providers without a company:** may they legally take paid bookings? We make no claim either way.
3. **Downtown Design:** the dates (about 4 to 8 Nov), whether Trove attends, and whether we may name the fair.
4. **Diwali:** is it on 8 Nov 2026? Check the greeting wording too.
5. **Founding maker:** can providers hold the title?
6. **Marketplace opening:** 1 Dec is conditional. Don't state it as certain (post 8).
7. **After 22 Nov:** do applications stay open? (Post 10.)
8. **Services as gifts from December:** is the feature ready? (Post 7.)
9. **"Look after the buyer"** (post 3): is it accurate?
10. **Arabic:** native MSA review of every line, plus a cultural check on talli and sadu.
11. **Trademark and usage check** in the UAE for "Made here. Made home." and "Home is handmade."
12. **Real people:** written consent, approved edits, neighbourhood only, and rights for the portrait photos.
13. **"Did someone send you?"** on /apply: does the field exist?
14. **Capacity:** staff for nominee DMs and the bring-one-thing sessions (venues, times).
15. **Link in bio** set to troveathome.com/apply, because caption links can't be clicked.
