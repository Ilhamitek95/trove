# Trove Phase 1: making the campaign concrete (v2)

Brand strategist, 19 Oct 2026. This answers the owner's note: "in the good direction, however it is a bit abstract". It is the plain-speaking layer and the people layer for the merged campaign in `posts.json` and `frames.js`. The campaign line "Made here. Made home." stays as the sign-off. It stops doing the explaining.

## 1. Why it reads abstract

The viewer is someone in Mirdif at 9pm who has never heard of Trove.

- **Nobody is there.** Every cover is an empty room, a doorway or tools on a table. In a feed of faces, a still life reads as an interiors brand or an estate agent, and her thumb doesn't stop.
- **She can't tell what Trove is.** "Trove" is an unknown word, and "Made here. Made home." could be a developer, a furniture shop or a candle brand. "A place to sell your handmade work or take bookings" turns up on slide 6 or in the caption, which almost nobody opens.
- **The headlines need a second read.** "This isn't instead", "Behind the door" and "Something of our own" rely on double meanings. That works in a deck. With one second of attention on a phone, a double meaning reads as no meaning.
- **The offer never appears.** No first frame says "sell", "bookings", "we photograph" or "courier". She never sees that someone wants to sell her cushions, so she doesn't recognise herself.
- **The ask comes last and is vague.** "Apply in about ten minutes" sits on the final slide, and "apply" sounds like a job. No Reel says what this is in its first two seconds.

## 2. The plain proposition

| # | Option (12 words or fewer) | Words | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Sell what you make. Take bookings for what you do. On Trove.** | 12 | Covers both sides in the viewer's own verbs. |
| B | **Make it at home. Trove photographs it, sells it and collects it.** | 12 | Shows what Trove does best, but leaves out providers. |
| C | **Gifted with your hands? Sell what you make on Trove, from home.** | 12 | The warmest of the three. Services are only implied. |

**Recommendation: A.** It is the only option that names both makers and providers. It needs no second read, and the viewer can see what she would do with it.

How the first frame is laid out:
- Above the sentence, a fixed small tag: **Dubai and Abu Dhabi**. The places are not counted in the 12 words.
- B's verbs do the explaining in post 3's subline.

**Arabic (MSA), marked for native review:** ما تصنعُه يداك يُباع، وموهبتُك تُحجَز، على Trove.
- It reads back as "What your hands make is sold, and your talent is booked, on Trove."
- Unvocalised, it reads for both men and women. "Trove" stays in Latin script.
- The reviewer may prefer a more direct imperative, such as "بِعْ ما تصنعه، واستقبِل حجوزات لما تُتقنه، على Trove". That form is masculine by default.

**First-frame stack for every post:**
1. Headline.
2. Subline.
3. Small footer strip: proposition A · troveathome.com/apply.

Every Reel shows the headline and the proposition within its first two seconds, and the voiceover's first sentence is the proposition.

## 4. Half-day "real people" shoot (this week)

**Who:**
- Three or four consenting real people from the team, friends and family who genuinely make or mend things. Use the real craft each person has. If nobody letters or restores, drop that shot rather than stage it.
- They are **never presented as Trove makers**. There are no name tags, and the only credit is "Filmed with friends of Trove at home in [area]".
- That changes only if a person later applies and is approved.

**Where:**
- Two homes, either one apartment and one villa or majlis, or one home and its building corridor.
- Shoot 14:00 to dusk (sunset is about 17:40, VERIFY) so the light ends warm, like evening at home.

**On set:**
- No house numbers, street signs, car plates or anything that places a home.
- No children's faces.
- No flag or emblem.
- Ask before filming in a majlis or any private room.
- Offer hands only to everyone up front, so nobody has to ask for it.

**Release and consent wording points** (ask a UAE lawyer to check the form, VERIFY; offer it in English and Arabic):
1. Who is filming: Trove, a brand of Serein Consultancy LLC. The date and place.
2. What is recorded: photos, video and voice. Ticks for: face, hands only, voice, first name shown, neighbourhood shown. Never the address.
3. Where it is used: Trove's Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, the website, paid ads (say so if yes), and printed postcards.
4. For how long: [two years], unless they withdraw.
5. Withdrawing: they can stop future use at any time, and Trove takes posts off its own channels within [7] days. Shares and printed items can't be recalled.
6. How they are described: never as a Trove maker or as selling on Trove unless they apply and are approved. No words they didn't say. They see the final edit before it is posted.
7. Their work stays theirs. Trove won't sell it or present it as Trove's. A credit or handle only if they want one.
8. No payment, and no link to applying. Nobody is obliged to apply, and saying no changes nothing.
9. The home: the person responsible for the home agrees to the interiors being filmed.
10. Copies: they receive the photos for personal use. Commercial use by them, for example if they later sell, is agreed separately.
11. Under-18s: not filmed. Hands only with a guardian's signature if it is ever needed.
12. Signature, date, and how the form is stored under Trove's Privacy Policy.
