# Trove Phase 1 social: the social-creative take

21 Oct to 17 Nov 2026. The objective: applications at troveathome.com/apply and nominations of gifted friends.

---

## 1. The angle

The UAE is home to people from everywhere, and what makes a flat or a villa here feel like home is often made by someone close by: a cousin's embroidery, a neighbour's bowl, the friend who sets every Eid table. Those people rarely apply for anything, because they don't see themselves as makers, let alone as a business; it is their friends who keep saying "you should sell these". So every post speaks to two people at once, the gifted one and the friend who believes in them, and every post is built to be sent, tagged or answered rather than just liked. Trove is where that sentence finally goes somewhere: from one home to another, in Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

**Candidate campaign lines**

1. **From one home to another.** This is the lead line. It is also literally how Trove works: the courier collects from one door here and delivers to another. AR: «من بيتٍ إلى بيت.» [AR, native review]
2. **Made by the people who live here.** AR: «بأيدي مَن يسكنون هنا.» [AR, native review]
3. **"You should sell these."** This is the friend's line, used as the recruitment hook. In Arabic, adapt it rather than translate it: «موهبتك تستحقّ بيتًا.» ("Your gift deserves a home") [AR, native review]

---

## 2. Recurring series

**"Just a hobby"** («مجرّد هواية»): a 30-second collab Reel about a real person here who is gifted with their hands and has never sold anything. Maker or provider, each one opens on "it's just a hobby", then shows why it isn't. Every other week, beyond Phase 1. **Shared because** it turns modesty into pride: friends fill the comments with "just a hobby?", the subject reposts it, and every thread becomes a list of other people's names.

**"Who made that?"** («من صنع هذا؟»): one handmade thing in a real home in Dubai or Abu Dhabi, and the person who made it. Followers join in through an Add Yours sticker and #WhoMadeThatTrove. **Shared because** crediting someone you love in public feels generous, and every tag is a nomination.

**"The house call"** («زيارة منزلية»): a provider at work in someone's home, such as a tablescape, a kintsugi repair, a workshop or an oud set in a majlis. From December it can show services bought as gifts. **Shared because** people watch a before-and-after to the end and send it to whoever is hosting next. Host and provider both repost.

## 5. Nomination mechanic: "Who's yours?"

1. **Ways in:** a comment tag on posts 1, 3, 4, 7 or 10; Add Yours or #WhoMadeThatTrove; a "Who should we meet?" Question sticker; a DM with a name.
2. **Log it** in one shared sheet: the nominee's handle, who nominated them, their craft or service, their area, the date and the status.
3. **Check it:** are they in Dubai or Abu Dhabi, and does their work fall in an accepted category? If not, reply warmly to the nominator only, and never DM the nominee.
4. **Thank the nominator:** like the comment and reply: "Thank you, we'll say hello."
5. **DM the nominee within two working days.** A person writes it, after looking at their work. Name the nominator only if they tagged publicly; otherwise ask the nominator first.
6. **Follow up once** after seven days if there's no reply, then stop.
7. **Close the loop:** if the nominee applies and agrees, tell the nominator. Willing nominees become "Just a hobby" or "Who made that?" subjects. A founding maker's spotlight thanks their nominator by name, with both people's consent. No paid rewards.

**DM to a nominee (56 words):**
> Hello [name], [nominator] tagged you on our post, and we've just spent a happy ten minutes looking at your [craft]. We're Trove, a new home for handmade pieces and creative services from people in Dubai and Abu Dhabi. No company needed to start [VERIFY]. If you're curious, troveathome.com/apply takes about ten minutes. No rush at all.
