# Trove Phase 1, v2: the concrete rebuild

Line: **Made here. Made home.** Dates, order and topics are the same as posts.json. Money appears only in post 10.

**How to read this file**
- `|` marks a line break.
- [Brackets] are real facts to fill in before posting.
- **REAL** frames are shot by the in-house photographer with consenting team members, friends or family. They are never presented as Trove makers, except in post 8, where the person is one.
- **MOOD** frames show props, tools and spaces only.

**What is new across all ten**
- Frame 1 of every post says plainly what Trove is and who it is for.
- Eight posts show the real website.
- Every post names real crafts and real steps.
- Five posts show hands or people.
