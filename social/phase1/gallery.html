Made here. Made home.

Trove's Phase 1 campaign to find makers and providers in Dubai and Abu Dhabi, from 21 October to 17 November. It talks about the platform and the people rather than the commission. It speaks to gifted and handy people who would never call themselves a business, and to the friends who keep telling them they should sell. It treats the UAE as home, made by the people who live here.

Merged from two marketing agents' proposals (a brand strategist and a social-first creative), written to the launch guidance. Their full work is in the Trove repo under social/phase1/agents.
