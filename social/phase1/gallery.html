Trove Phase 1 creatives

Version 6, a draft: nothing is scheduled or posted. Thirteen posts to find makers and providers in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah, planned for 31 October to 28 November, then the buyers Reel on 2 December. Same order as before: what Trove is, who it's for (makers, providers and buyers), then posts of their own for makers and for providers, money, a founding maker and the Collection. After the 22 October feedback and the owner's ask for more images and video, less text, eight of the fourteen posts are Reels (2, 3, 5, 8, 10, 12, 13, 14) cut from Higgsfield clips of hands at work, and every carousel slide is a photo with one short line; the captions do the explaining. Each post has its own lead colour, and the pieces shown cover every kind of maker, bright and patterned as well as quiet. Higgsfield images and video are stand-ins (hands only, Instagram AI label on) until the real shoot replaces them. Reels marked new in v6 play once the producer has generated and cut them.

v6 follows the 22 October feedback (social/phase1/FEEDBACK-2026-10-22.md); the plan and the producer's asset list are in social/phase1/V6-PLAN.md. v5 was rebuilt from v4 after the 20 October creative review, checked against the brief and the live site. The marketing agents' earlier proposals are in the Trove repo under social/phase1/agents.
