# Trove social brief: finding makers and providers

Shared brief for the Trove marketing agents (`.claude/agents/trove-brand-strategist.md`, `.claude/agents/trove-social-creative.md`). Read all of it before writing a word.

## The job

Phase 1 of the launch (21 Oct to 22 Nov 2026) has one objective: **find founding makers and providers** before the Marketplace opens to buyers (1 Dec 2026, if enough makers are ready). Success is applications at troveathome.com/apply, plus people nominating gifted friends. Likes do not count.

## The owner's direction (19 Oct 2026, overrides the earlier plan)

1. **Talk about the platform and the people, not the commission.** The earlier drafts led with "keep 60%" and lines lifted from the website. The money is a reassurance detail for a caption, a Story or an FAQ answer. It is never the headline, and at most one post in ten leans on it.
2. **Speak to people who are gifted with their hands, including those with no company.** Makers, yes. But also the handy people who have never sold anything and would never call themselves a business: the neighbour whose embroidery everyone asks for, the father who restores furniture at weekends, the friend whose candles are at every dinner, the aunt who does the calligraphy for every wedding card, the person who styles every family party, the hobby photographer. The same goes for providers: people who teach, style, shoot, repair or perform.
3. **Support the people of the UAE.** Producers, makers, home-based talent, and everyone who lives here. **The UAE as home:** warmth, homeliness, the things that turn a house into a home, made by people who live here. Trove is a home for that work.
4. **Think like marketers, not copy extractors.** Find the human insight, the emotional idea and the mechanic that makes someone apply or nominate a friend. Do not lift lines from the website. Facts below keep you truthful; they are not copy.

## Who Trove is (facts: keep everything you write true to these)

- **Trove** (troveathome.com), tagline "Curated for Living". A curated home for handmade pieces and creative services from independents in the UAE. Two sides: the **Trove Marketplace** (makers' pieces and providers' services) and the **Trove Collection** (Trove's own house range, tease only, no reveal before 2027).
- A brand of Serein Consultancy LLC. Not a charity: no social-impact numbers or claims of "giving back".
- **Where:** Dubai and Abu Dhabi only. The courier collects from makers' doors there and delivers to buyers there. Never promise other emirates.
- **Makers' pieces Trove accepts:** Home & Living, Wall Art & Prints, Ceramics, Stationery, Jewellery (non-piercing), Personalised Gifts, Children's, Pet Accessories, Wellness (eye pillows, wheat bags, never skincare), Accessories, Seasonal, Handmade Crafts. **Not accepted:** food or drink, cosmetics, skincare, soap, perfume, supplements.
- **Providers' services** (in person in Dubai and Abu Dhabi):
  - **Made to order:** murals, calligraphy and hand-lettering, embroidery and monogramming, memory quilts, portraits.
  - **Care and repair:** kintsugi, furniture restoration, curtains, rug repair.
  - **Styling and celebrations:** tablescapes; seasonal home styling for Ramadan, Eid, Diwali, Christmas and National Day; gift wrapping; party setups.
  - **Workshops at home:** pottery, embroidery, candle pouring, calligraphy, kids' craft birthdays.
  - **Portraits and photography at home.**
  - **Live creative entertainment:** storytelling, oud sets, poetry and calligraphy performances.
  - **Services for makers:** product photography, brand and packaging design, English–Arabic copy, social media, selling support, coaching.
  - **Not accepted:** food or catering, beauty or skin treatments, medical or financial services.
- **True for makers** (reassurance, never the lead):
  - No trade licence or company needed **to start**. Trove buys each piece and resells it. Always say "to start", never "ever". A UAE business adviser must confirm this before it is posted, so flag it.
  - Trove checks the maker's Emirates ID and home address before the first payout. It may ask for an e-Trader licence if sales grow a lot.
  - Nothing to join, and nothing to pay if nothing sells.
  - The maker sets the price and keeps 60% when a piece sells.
  - Trove's photographer photographs the pieces, or makers can use their own photos.
  - A courier collects from the maker's door. Makers pack in their own packaging.
  - Payouts come every other Tuesday, after the buyer's 15-day return window. Never say "instant".
  - Four lovely pieces is a shop. Makers choose how many orders a month they can handle.
  - Not exclusive: makers keep their Instagram, markets and everything else.
- **True for providers:**
  - Free to list during launch (AED 30 a month later, with 30 days' notice).
  - When the customer pays the provider directly, the provider keeps 100%. Paid by card through Trove, they keep 90%.
  - Booking requests arrive in one dashboard.
  - From December, services can be given as gifts.
- **Applying:** about ten minutes at troveathome.com/apply. A real person reads every application, usually within a day or two. One account can sell pieces and offer services.
- **No real makers are live yet.** There is a Founding Makers programme: the first 20 to 30 get the name "Founding maker", a badge on their shop and a spotlight.
- The owner has an **in-house photographer**, so real photo and video shoots of makers cost hours, not money.

## Rules (non-negotiable)

- **Language:** UK English, no emoji. "Trove" is always capitalised in copy.
- **No urgency or scarcity:** nothing like "hurry", "limited spots", "last chance", "don't miss out", or countdowns. The brand's own line is "Have a wander. There's no rush."
- **No superiority claims:** no "first", "only", "largest" or "the UAE's Etsy". Other UAE handmade marketplaces exist (Sparkleberry, Crafty UAE). Never knock other platforms or "mass-produced" goods. Show the real thing instead.
- **Never invent people or numbers:** no made-up makers, quotes, testimonials, statistics or applicant counts. Use [brackets] wherever a real person or fact is needed.
- **AI imagery:** for mood (materials, tools, spaces), Collection teases and, since 21 Oct 2026, stand-in covers of hands at work until the shoot. Never present it as a maker's work and never invent a named person. Real makers' hands, faces and pieces come from real photos and video.
- **Culture:** speak to Emiratis and to the many nationalities who call the UAE home, inclusively and respectfully.
  - Do not use the UAE flag, the national emblem, or leaders' images or quotes in commercial posts.
  - Do not quote official slogans or "Year of ..." themes unless certain. Mark anything like that **VERIFY**.
- **Arabic:** suggest Arabic lines (Modern Standard Arabic) where they would help reach Arabic-speaking makers, and mark them for native review.
- **Voice:** warm, calm, plain-spoken, quietly poetic, never salesy or corporate. Short sentences. No buzzwords ("empower", "unleash", "ecosystem", "passion-driven", "artisanal journey").

## Formats and calendar

- **Channels:** Instagram first. Facebook and TikTok where it is natural.
- **Formats:** Reels (9:16), carousels (4:5), single images, Stories, collab posts with makers, DMs.
- **Schedule:** one grid post every three days at 18:30 Dubai time. Since v5 (20 Oct): eleven posts, 21, 24, 27, 30 Oct and 2, 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20 Nov. Stories run in between. The dates are a plan only: nothing is scheduled or posted until the owner says launch.
- **Cover rules (from the 20 Oct creative review, v5):** say what Trove is first, then who it's for (makers, providers, buyers). One headline of seven words or fewer, plus one short line, with the offer visible ("Free to join · We photograph it · We collect it"). Every cover shows hands at work or real work, or is a plain type card. **Owner, 21 Oct 2026:** until the shoot, photo covers may use Higgsfield images (hands only, no faces, never captioned as a named maker's work, Instagram AI label on); each is swapped for the real shot once it is taken. "Made here. Made home." is an end-card sign-off only. One-liner: "Trove is a new online marketplace for handmade pieces and creative services in Dubai and Abu Dhabi."
- **Downtown Design:** in early November (around 4–8 Nov, VERIFY). Maker outreach happens in person there too.
- **Call to action:** every post points to troveathome.com/apply.
