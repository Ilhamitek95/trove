# Trove Seller Agreement

**Version v5 · 2 October 2026 · Serein Consultancy LLC, trading as Trove · Sharjah Media City (Shams), United Arab Emirates**

This agreement is between you (the **supplier**) and **Serein Consultancy
LLC**, a limited liability company licensed by Sharjah Media City (Shams),
licence no. 2220356.01. Trove and Trove at Home are its brand names; in this
agreement, **Trove** means Serein Consultancy LLC. It is written in plain
English on purpose — this is genuinely how it works.

## 1. How selling on Trove works

Trove is not a middleman holding your money. Trove **buys your goods from you
and resells them** to its customers:

- You list your pieces on Trove with the price you want them to sell at (your
  **list price**).
- When a customer orders one of your pieces and their payment succeeds,
  **Trove purchases that piece from you at that moment**. Ownership (title) of
  the goods transfers to Trove at order confirmation.
- Trove is the **seller of record** to the customer. The customer buys from
  Trove, pays Trove, and holds Trove responsible for the sale.

## 2. What Trove pays you

- Your **purchase price** for each piece is your list price **minus a 40%
  margin** kept by Trove.
- Purchases are settled **fortnightly, every other Tuesday**, by bank
  transfer to the IBAN you provide — which must be an account **in your own
  name**.
- A purchase becomes payable after the piece is **delivered to the customer
  and the customer's 15-day return window has closed**, with no return of it
  still in progress. Until then it shows in your dashboard as pending.
- The bank transfer reference will read **“Purchase of handmade goods”**
  followed by a purchase-order number. For each settlement Trove issues you a
  purchase note — the record of what Trove bought from you and what it paid.
  You do not need to invoice Trove.

## 3. Returns and refunds

- Because Trove is the seller of record, **customer refunds, returns and
  payment disputes are Trove's to handle**, at Trove's cost of administration.
- Customers can ask to return a piece within **15 days of delivery**. A
  personalised piece can only be returned if it is faulty or damaged, the
  wrong item, or not as described.
- When Trove accepts a return, its courier collects the piece from the
  customer and brings it back to you, and the customer is refunded once the
  courier has collected it. Trove's purchase of the returned pieces is
  reversed: title reverts to you, and the purchase price of just those pieces
  (or units) comes off what Trove owes you. Any return collection fee is
  Trove's and the customer's matter — it is never charged to you.
- Because a purchase only becomes payable after the return window, a return
  normally reverses a purchase that has not been paid yet. If a refund does
  come after a settlement, the amount is deducted from your next settlement.
  If that leaves your balance negative, the difference carries forward and
  nets against your future settlements — Trove will never take money back out
  of your bank account mid-cycle.

## 4. What you promise about your goods

You warrant that everything you list on Trove:

- is **handmade by you** (or your small team) and your own original work —
  not counterfeit, not a replica, and not carrying a brand you don't own or
  license;
- is **yours to sell** — no stolen, borrowed or third-party stock;
- is **safe for its intended use**, **lawful to sell** in the United Arab
  Emirates and accurately described and photographed;
- is **not** in a prohibited category: nothing ingestible and nothing applied
  to the skin — no food, beverages, cosmetics, skincare, soap, perfume,
  candles marketed for skin contact, or supplements.

## 5. Verifying who you are

Trove buys goods from you, so Trove must know who you are.

- Settlements are paid only once your identity is verified: either a **UAE
  trade or e-Trader license** that Trove has verified, or, if you sell
  without one, clear photos of the **front and back of your Emirates ID**
  (with its last four digits and expiry date) that Trove's administrator
  has checked, and your **home address**.
- Your Emirates ID must stay **in date**. Trove emails you **30 days before
  it expires**. While it has expired, settlements to you **pause**; nothing
  you are owed is lost, and paused settlements are paid in the next run
  after you upload your renewed card and it has been checked.
- The ID photos are stored **encrypted**; your ID details, address and
  license document are visible only to Trove's administrator, and are used
  only to verify your identity, to meet Trove's legal obligations, and to
  respond to lawful requests from UAE authorities. They are kept while you
  sell on Trove and for five years after your shop closes, then deleted, as
  the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) explains.
- Everything you provide — identity, address, bank details — must be **your
  own, current and truthful**. Selling under someone else's identity ends
  this agreement immediately.
- If the settlements Trove pays you in any 30-day period reach the licence
  threshold stated in the maker section of the [Help centre](/faq#makers)
  (AED 12,000 when this version was published), you will be asked to
  obtain a **UAE e-Trader or trade license** to keep scaling. Trove will
  guide you through it; with a verified license you can move to direct
  per-sale payouts.

## 6. Your accountability

- **You are accountable for the goods you supply.** If Trove suffers a loss
  because your goods or your information broke a promise in this agreement —
  a fine, a legal claim, a product-safety recall, or a customer dispute
  caused by counterfeit, unsafe or misdescribed goods — you are responsible
  for compensating Trove for that loss.
- While Trove investigates a genuine concern about your goods or identity, it
  may **pause your shop** and **hold back the settlement of the purchases
  under investigation**. Settlements resume (or reverse, under section 3) when
  the investigation closes.
- You will answer Trove's **reasonable questions** about your goods and
  identity when UAE law or a marketplace obligation requires it.
- This section works both ways in spirit: nothing in it makes you responsible
  for things Trove gets wrong — Trove remains responsible to its customers as
  the seller of record, and to you under this agreement.

## 7. Practical points

- Before your shop is approved, you accept this agreement and give Trove a
  **collection (pickup) address and phone** for the courier. Trove's courier
  collects sold pieces from you there; you'll see pickup details on each
  order in your dashboard.
- You see the buyer's name on each order. You see a buyer's **full delivery
  address** only for a parcel you deliver yourself, and only until that
  order's return window closes. Use it only to deliver that order, never
  keep or share it, and never contact the buyer for anything else.
- Trove may update this agreement; you'll be asked to accept a new version
  before it applies to your future sales. If a new version changes how or
  when you are paid, your pieces are taken off sale until you accept it, so
  nothing is ever sold to Trove on terms you have not agreed.
- Either side can end this agreement at any time; purchases already made are
  settled normally under it.
