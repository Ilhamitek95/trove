- The provider is responsible for holding any licence, permit or insurance
  their work needs, and for delivering the service safely and as described.

## 2. How booking works

- You send a **booking request** with your name, email, UAE mobile, area
  and a note about what you have in mind. No account is needed: we email you
  a **private link** to your booking, where you can check its progress and
  cancel it. Keep that email, and do not share the link.
- The provider **confirms or declines**. Until your booking is secured, your
  contact details stay with Trove. Once it is secured — when the provider
  confirms a booking you settle directly, or when your card payment is
  received for a booking you pay through Trove — your **mobile number** is
  released to the provider so they can arrange the details with you. Your
  **email address is never shared** with the provider.
- The details of the service — time, place, final scope and anything not
  covered by the listing — are agreed between you and the provider.
- If the service happens at your home, please make sure the space is safe
  and accessible, and that a parent or guardian is present throughout for
  anything involving children.

## 3. Paying

You choose how to pay when you send the request.

**Settle directly with the provider.** The payment is between you and the
provider — bank transfer, cash on the day, or whatever you agree. Trove takes
no part in it, receives nothing from it and **cannot refund it**. Agree any
deposit or cancellation terms with the provider before the service.

**Pay through Trove by card.** Nothing is charged when you send the request.

- When the provider confirms, they set the **service date** and the
  **price of the booking**: the listed price for a fixed-price service, or
  the final price for a service listed with a starting price or per hour
  (a starting price is never lowered below the one listed). We email you the
  amount and a secure payment link.
- You pay Trove that amount by card. Your booking is **secured once the
  payment is received**. If the date or price isn't right for you, don't
  pay — cancel from your private link instead; nothing is charged.
- For that booking, Trove is your contracting party and engages the provider
  as its independent contractor to deliver the service.
- If the service is **not delivered**, Trove refunds you in full.
- If something goes wrong with a service you paid for through Trove, tell
  Trove within **7 days** of the service date. Trove will look at it
  reasonably and decide what, if anything, is refunded. Trove's total
  liability for a booking is **limited to the amount you paid** for it.
- Paying through Trove does not make Trove the provider of the service or
  responsible for how it is performed. Section 5 still applies.

Listed prices are the provider's own. Trove adds nothing on top.

## 4. Cancelling

- You can cancel a booking at any time **before the service date** — from
  **Your bookings** on the Services Marketplace if you are signed in, or from
  the private link in your emails. On or after the service date, contact
  Trove.
- If you paid through Trove and cancel before the service date, you are
  **refunded in full**, back to the card you paid with.
- For direct bookings, any late-cancellation or deposit terms are the ones
  you agreed with the provider.
- Providers can decline or cancel too. If a booking you paid through Trove is
  declined or cancelled by the provider, or by Trove, you are refunded in

- Refunds go back to the original card. Depending on your bank they can take
  5–10 business days to appear.
