# Trove Provider Agreement

**Version v1 · Trove, Dubai, United Arab Emirates**

This agreement is between you (the **provider**) and Trove. It covers the
Services Marketplace — the part of Trove where customers book services from
independent providers. It is written in plain English on purpose. Please
read it: the most important point is in section 2, and it is that **the
services you offer are yours, not Trove's**.

## 1. What Trove is — and is not

- Trove is a curated marketplace. It lists your services, passes booking
  requests to you, and gives you a dashboard to manage them.
- Trove is **not the provider of your services**. Trove does not perform,
  supervise, direct or control your work, and it does not employ you.
- You are an **independent business**. Nothing in this agreement makes you an
  employee, agent, partner or representative of Trove, and you must not
  present yourself as one.
- Trove reviews every provider before listing them. That review is about
  the quality of your work as shown to us; it is not an inspection,
  certification or guarantee of your services.

## 2. Your services are your responsibility

- **You alone are responsible for every service you deliver**: its quality,
  safety, timing, materials and results, and your conduct at a customer's
  home, studio or event.
- You are responsible for holding any **licence, permit, registration,
  visa or insurance** that your work requires under UAE law, and for your
  own **taxes**, including VAT on your services if you are registered.
- Regulated work is outside the Services Marketplace: no food or catering,
  no beauty or skin treatments, no medical, legal or financial services
  beyond plain advice, and nothing that needs a professional licence you do
  not hold. Trove may remove any listing it considers regulated or unsafe.
- Your listings must be honest: what is included, what it costs, how long it
  takes and where it happens must match what you actually deliver.
- Where a service involves children, you will make sure a parent or
  guardian is present throughout. Where it involves a customer's home, you
  will treat it, and the people in it, with care.

## 3. Bookings and the customer's details

- Customers send **booking requests**. You choose which to confirm; you can
  decline any request, with or without a note, and there is no penalty for
  declining.
- Until you confirm, Trove keeps the customer's contact details private.
  Confirming releases the customer's **mobile number** so you can arrange
  the visit. The customer's **email address stays with Trove** and is never
  shared with you.
- You will use the customer's details only to deliver that booking — not for
  marketing, and not to pass to anyone else.
- Confirmed bookings are commitments. If you cannot deliver one, tell the
  customer as early as you can and mark it in your dashboard. Repeated
  no-shows or late cancellations can lead to suspension.

## 4. Money

**The subscription.** Being listed costs a flat **AED 30 a month**. It starts
on the day your profile is first approved and runs month to month. It is
billed by card once Trove's card payments launch; you will be told before
the first charge. There are no listing fees.

**Direct settlement.** When a customer chooses to settle directly with you,
the payment is entirely between you and the customer — bank transfer, cash,
or whatever you agree. Trove is not part of that payment, takes nothing from
it, and cannot refund it. Any cancellation or deposit terms for direct
bookings are for you to agree with the customer before the service.

**Paying through Trove.** When a customer chooses to pay through Trove:

- The customer pays Trove the listed price for the booking, by card, once
  you have confirmed it. Trove is the customer's contracting party for that
  booking, and **engages you as an independent contractor** to deliver it.
- Trove keeps a **platform fee of 10%** of the amount the customer paid, and
  **pays you the remaining 90%** as your fee for delivering the service.
- Your fee becomes payable once you have marked the booking done, and is
  paid in Trove's regular settlement run by bank transfer to an account
  **in your own name**. Trove issues you a note for each settlement; you do
  not need to invoice Trove.
- If the service is not delivered, Trove refunds the customer and nothing is
  payable to you. If a customer raises a dispute about a Trove-paid booking,
  Trove will look at it reasonably and decide what, if anything, is
  refunded; where a refund is made, your fee is reduced by the same share.
- Trove's platform fee is inclusive of VAT where applicable. You remain
  responsible for any VAT due on your own fee.

**No steering.** You may accept direct bookings freely, but you must not use
Trove's marketplace to move a customer who chose to pay through Trove onto a
direct arrangement in order to avoid the platform fee.

## 5. Liability

- To the fullest extent permitted by UAE law, **Trove is not liable for your
  services** or for anything that arises from them: injury, illness, damage
  to property, loss, delay, disappointment, or any dispute between you and a
  customer.
- You will **indemnify Trove** — meaning you will cover Trove's losses,
  costs and reasonable legal fees — against any claim by a customer or a
  third party that arises from your services, your conduct or your breach
  of this agreement.
- Trove's total liability to you under this agreement, for any reason, is
  limited to the subscription fees you paid Trove in the three months before
  the claim.
- Nothing in this agreement excludes or limits any liability that cannot be
  excluded or limited under UAE law.

## 6. Suspension and ending

- Trove may suspend or remove your profile or any listing at any time for
  customer complaints, safety concerns, misrepresentation, regulated work,
  breach of this agreement, or anything that could bring Trove into
  disrepute. Where practical, Trove will tell you why.
- You may stop at any time from your dashboard or by contacting Trove. Your
  listings come down, your subscription ends at the end of the current
  month, and confirmed bookings should still be honoured or cancelled with
  the customer directly.
- Sections 2, 4 (for bookings already made) and 5 continue to apply after
  this agreement ends.

## 7. Practical points

- Trove may update this agreement. You will be told of material changes
  before they apply; continuing to use the Services Marketplace after that
  means you accept them. The version you accepted, and when, is recorded on
  your profile.
- This agreement is governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates as
  applied in the Emirate of Dubai, and the courts of Dubai have exclusive
  jurisdiction.
- If any part of this agreement is found unenforceable, the rest still
  applies.

**By applying to the Services Marketplace, or by switching on services from
your Trove dashboard, you accept this agreement.**
