# Trove Privacy Policy

**Version v2 · Last updated 2 October 2026 · Serein Consultancy LLC, trading as Trove · Sharjah Media City (Shams), United Arab Emirates**

This policy explains what personal data Trove collects when you use
troveathome.com, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and the
rights you have. It is written to meet the UAE Personal Data Protection Law
(Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021, the **PDPL**) and is in plain English on
purpose.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

Trove and Trove at Home are brand names of **Serein Consultancy LLC**, a
limited liability company licensed by Sharjah Media City (Shams), licence
no. 2220356.01. Serein Consultancy LLC is the **controller** of the personal
data described here; in this policy, **Trove**, **we** and **us** mean that
company. Its details are also on the [About page](/about). To ask anything
about your data, or to use any of your rights, write to us through the
[Contact page](/contact) and choose **Privacy and my data**.

## 2. What we collect

**When you shop**

- Your **name, email address and password** (the password is stored only as
  a one-way hash, never in readable form). If you sign in with Google, we
  receive your name and email address from Google.
- Your **UAE mobile number**, which the courier needs for every delivery.
- Your **delivery addresses**.
- Your **orders**: what you bought, prices, delivery and return history,
  and any return request, including the reason and photos you upload.
- **Payment**: card payments are handled by Stripe. We receive a payment
  reference and the payment's status. Trove does not offer saved cards, and
  **we never see or store your card number.**
- **Reviews** you write, and messages you send us through the contact form.
- If you check out as a **guest** and you have, or later open, an account
  with the same email address, your guest orders are added to that account
  once the email address has been confirmed, so you can follow and return
  them there.

**When you book a service**

- The name, email, mobile number, area and note you put in a booking
  request. The provider sees your name, area, date and note; your mobile
  number is released to them only once they confirm; your email address is
  never shared with the provider.

**When you sell or offer services on Trove (makers and providers)**

- Your application: name, email, mobile or WhatsApp number, shop or practice
  details, links and what you make.
- Your **collection (pickup) address and phone**, which we need before your
  shop is approved, shared only with the courier.
- Your **bank details** (account name, bank and IBAN), used only to pay
  you. The IBAN is stored **encrypted**; everywhere else it is shown masked.
- If you sell without a UAE trade licence: photos of the **front and back of
  your Emirates ID**, its **last four digits**, its **expiry date** and, if
  you give it, its **issue date**, and your **home address**. The ID photos
  are stored **encrypted**. All of it is seen only by Trove's administrator
  and used only to verify who you are and to meet Trove's legal
  obligations. The
  last four digits of your Emirates ID are printed on the purchase notes
  Trove issues you for each settlement. We use the expiry date to email you
  30 days before your Emirates ID expires and to pause settlements while it
  has expired, until you upload the renewed card. If you have a trade licence, the
  licence number and the licence document you upload, kept in private
  storage that is never published and only Trove's administrator can open.

**When you browse**

- A **session cookie** that keeps you signed in and your checkout secure.
  It is essential; the site cannot work without it.
- Your **basket**, kept in your own browser (local storage) so it survives
  a refresh.
- An **anonymous visitor id**, a random code kept in your browser's local
  storage, **only if you agree to measurement cookies**. When you open a
  shop or a piece, or add a piece to your basket, we record that action
  with this id so makers can see how their shop is doing, in totals only.
  Until you agree, no id is stored or sent; if you refuse, or later
  withdraw your agreement, the id is deleted from your browser. The id is
  not linked to your name or account, it is removed from our records after
  **90 days**, and the records themselves are deleted after **a year**.
- The **words you search for**, stored without any link to you for **90
  days**, to improve search results.
- Standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type), kept briefly
  for security and to keep the site running.
- Your **cookie choice**. On your first visit a banner asks whether you
  agree to measurement cookies. Nothing optional runs until you say yes,
  wherever you are, and you can change your mind any time with **Cookie
  settings** at the bottom of every page. The choice is recorded by
  **iubenda** (our consent tool) in a cookie in your browser and kept for
  **180 days**, with a record of the choice kept as proof of consent.
- **Google Analytics**, through **Google Tag Manager**, a script from
  Google that our pages load. It runs only if you agree to measurement
  cookies: until then it sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser
  and sends nothing to Google Analytics. Like any file your browser
  fetches, loading Tag Manager shares your IP address and browser details
  with Google. Tag Manager and Google Analytics are not loaded at all on
  your account pages, the maker and provider dashboards, the admin panel or
  private links (such as a password reset or a booking link).

We do not use advertising or marketing cookies. If we ever add any, we will
update this policy first and they will run only if you agree to them. We do
not sell your personal data.

## 3. Why we use it (and our legal basis)

- **To sell you what you order and deliver it**: processing your order,
  taking payment, booking the courier, handling returns and refunds, and
  sending order and return emails. This is necessary to perform our
  contract with you.
- **To pay makers and providers and verify who they are**: necessary for
  our contract with them and to meet legal obligations.
- **To keep Trove safe**: preventing fraud and abuse, rate-limiting, and
  keeping backups. This is in our legitimate interest and yours.
- **To meet legal obligations**: keeping accounting, tax and commercial
  records, and responding to lawful requests from UAE authorities.
- **To improve Trove**: anonymous search statistics, and shop statistics
  only where you have agreed to measurement cookies (your consent).
- We will ask for your **consent** before using your data for anything else,
  such as marketing emails, and you can withdraw consent at any time.

## 4. Who we share it with

We share only what each service needs, under contracts that require them to
protect it and use it only to provide their service to Trove:

- **Stripe** (card payments and refunds).
- **OTO**, the courier platform that books our deliveries, and the courier
  companies it assigns (your name, mobile number and delivery address for
  your parcel; a maker's collection address and phone). If OTO is
  unavailable we may use **Quiqup** in the same way.
- **Resend** (sending our transactional emails, such as receipts).
- **Render** (the hosting provider whose servers run troveathome.com and
  hold its database and backups).
- **Google** (if you choose "Sign in with Google"; the site's fonts are
  loaded from Google Fonts and our public pages load Google Tag Manager,
  both of which receive your IP address; Google Analytics receives nothing
  unless you agree to measurement cookies).
- **iubenda** (our cookie banner: it shows the banner, records your cookie
  choice and passes it to Google's consent settings).
- **Anthropic** (only for makers who use the optional AI tag-writer: the
  product name, description, category and shop name are sent to suggest
  search tags. No buyer data is sent.)
- **Our bank**, which carries out the transfers that pay makers and
  providers (the account holder's name, IBAN, the amount and a reference).
  Provider fees are paid by bank transfer from Serein Consultancy LLC, the
  company that runs Trove, so that name appears on a provider's statement.
- **Makers and providers** see only what they need: a maker sees the pieces
  ordered from their shop and the name on the order, and sees your full
  delivery address only for a parcel the maker delivers by hand, and only
  until that order's return window closes; a maker never sees your email
  address or mobile number. A provider sees what is described in section
  2.
- **Authorities**, where UAE law requires it.

Some of these services are based outside the UAE, so your data may be
processed in other countries (for example the United States or the European
Union). Where that happens, we rely on the safeguards the PDPL allows,
including contractual protections with each provider.

## 5. How long we keep it

- **Account data** (name, email, mobile, password, addresses): while your
  account is open. When you ask us to close it, we close and anonymise it
  as described below.
- **Orders, payments, refunds and settlements**: at least five years, as
  UAE tax and commercial record-keeping rules require. If you close your
  account, these records are kept without your name, email address, mobile
  number or delivery address.
- **Return request photos**: one year after the return is closed (refunded
  or declined), then deleted automatically. The record of the return and its
  refund is kept with the order.
- **Emirates ID photos, Emirates ID digits and dates, home address and
  licence document (makers)**: while you sell on Trove, and for five years
  after your shop is closed, then deleted automatically. We can delete them
  sooner on request where no law requires us to keep them.
- **Bank details (IBAN)**: while you sell or provide services on Trove. They
  are deleted when your account is closed; the masked number and bank name
  stay on past settlement records.
- **Contact form messages**: two years from when they were sent, then
  deleted automatically.
- **The words you search for**: 90 days.
- **Anonymous visitor ids**: 90 days; the anonymous event records: one
  year.
- **Backups**: rolling nightly copies, each kept for seven days, so
  anything deleted also leaves our backups within seven days.

**Closing your account.** When you ask us to close your account, we first
make sure nothing is still in progress (an order not yet delivered or still
inside its return window, an open return, an upcoming booking, or money
still owed to or by a maker or provider); we will tell you if something
needs to finish first. Then we:

- replace your name, email address and mobile number with a placeholder and
  make the account impossible to sign in to, and sign it out everywhere;
- delete your saved addresses, the reviews you wrote and your contact form
  messages;
- remove your name, email address, mobile number and delivery address from
  your past orders and bookings, any personalisation text and the details
  and photos of your return requests, keeping only the amounts, dates and
  pieces that the record-keeping rules above require; and
- for makers and providers, take the shop or practice off Trove, delete the
  application details, collection address and phone, and bank details, and
  keep identity documents only for the period stated above.

## 6. How we protect it

Connections to troveathome.com are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed.
Bank account numbers (IBANs) and Emirates ID images are encrypted at rest.
Licence documents are kept in private storage that is never published.
Only Trove's administrator can see any of them, and signing in to the
administrator account needs a one-time code sent by email as well as the
password. Access to the database is limited to the people who run Trove. No system is perfectly secure, but we
work to protect your data and will tell you and the authorities about a
breach where the law requires.

## 7. Your rights

Under the PDPL you have the right to:

- **know** what personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
- have it **corrected** if it is wrong or incomplete;
- have it **erased**, where we no longer need it or have no legal reason to
  keep it;
- **restrict** or **object to** how we use it in some cases;
- receive it in a structured, machine-readable form (**portability**); and
- **withdraw consent** where we rely on it.

To use any of these rights, write to us through the [Contact page](/contact)
and choose **Privacy and my data**. We may need to confirm who you are first
(usually by replying from the email address on your account). We will reply
within 30 days. A **copy of your data** is sent as a structured,
machine-readable file (JSON) covering your account, addresses, orders,
returns, reviews, bookings and messages, and, for makers and providers, your
shop or practice details. An **erasure** request is carried out by closing
and anonymising your account as described in section 5. If
you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the UAE Data
Office.
