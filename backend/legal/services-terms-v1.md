## 2. How booking works

## 3. Paying

You choose how to pay when you send the request.

**Settle directly with the provider.** The payment is between you and the
provider — bank transfer, cash on the day, or whatever you agree. Trove takes

Listed prices are the provider's own. Where a price is shown as a starting
price or per hour, the final amount depends on what you agree with the
